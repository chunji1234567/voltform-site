Voltform

The companion app for your smart charger. 智能充电器的配套 App。

What it does

  • Connects to your charger over Bluetooth — no account, no internet needed.
  • Live voltage, current, power and charger temperature.
  • Charge curves kept through the whole charge, and a report when it ends.
  • Adjust target voltage, current and battery type, protected by your charger's passwords.
  • Available in English, 中文, Русский, Français, Español, Deutsch, Português, 日本語, 한국어.

功能

  • 通过蓝牙连接充电器，无需注册账号，无需联网。
  • 实时查看电压、电流、功率和机内温度。
  • 整个充电过程的曲线完整保存，充电结束生成报告。
  • 修改目标电压、电流和电池类型，由充电器上的密码保护。
