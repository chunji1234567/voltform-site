Privacy Policy

Voltform app · Effective 25 November 2026

Voltform is published by Tianchang Nuorui Electronics Co., Ltd ("we"). This policy explains what the Voltform app does with information. In short: the app does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything stays on your phone. We have no servers and no access to it.

  • Charge data: voltage, current and temperature readings, charge curves and charge reports, kept per charger so a charge can continue after you reconnect. Curves unused for 7 days are deleted automatically; up to 15 reports are kept per charger.
  • Charger identity: the charger's name and its fixed ID (read from the charger), used to tell your chargers apart.
  • Preferences: language, light or dark theme, and reduced motion.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Passwords

Your charger's connection and voltage passwords are sent only to the charger over Bluetooth to unlock it. They are never saved to storage. The voltage password is kept in memory for the current connection only and is cleared when you disconnect.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to find and connect to your charger and read its status. The app does not use Bluetooth to locate you.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only): these Android versions require it for any Bluetooth scan. The app does not read or record your location.
  • Notifications (Android): shows an ongoing notification while the app keeps the charger connected in the background.
  • Background Bluetooth (iPhone): keeps receiving charger data while the app is in the background, so the charge curve stays complete.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No connection to the internet for app features.

Children

The app is a tool for charger owners and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version adds online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

隐私政策

Voltform App · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 25 日

Voltform 由天长市诺锐电子有限公司（“我们”）发布。本政策说明 Voltform App 如何处理信息。简单来说：本 App 不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。

App 保存什么、保存在哪里

所有数据只保存在你的手机上。我们没有服务器，也无法访问这些数据。

  • 充电数据：电压、电流、温度读数，充电曲线和充电报告，按充电器分别保存，重新连接后可接着记录。7 天未使用的曲线自动删除；每台充电器最多保留 15 份报告。
  • 充电器标识：充电器名称和固定 ID（从充电器读取），用于区分你的多台充电器。
  • 偏好设置：语言、浅色或深色主题、减少动态效果。

删除 App 即删除以上全部数据。

密码

充电器的连接密码和电压密码只通过蓝牙发送给充电器用于验证，从不保存到存储中。电压密码只在本次连接期间保存在内存里，断开连接即清除。

权限

  • 蓝牙：用于搜索、连接充电器并读取状态，不用于定位你。
  • 位置（仅 Android 11 及以下）：这些 Android 版本进行蓝牙搜索时系统要求此权限。App 不读取、不记录你的位置。
  • 通知（Android）：App 在后台保持连接充电器时显示一条常驻通知。
  • 后台蓝牙（iPhone）：App 在后台时继续接收充电器数据，保证充电曲线完整。

我们不做的事

  • 不需要注册账号或登录。
  • 不使用统计分析、广告或追踪，不集成收集数据的第三方 SDK。
  • App 的功能不需要联网。

儿童

本 App 是供充电器用户使用的工具，不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页及生效日期。如果以后的版本加入联网功能，会在该版本发布前更新本政策。
